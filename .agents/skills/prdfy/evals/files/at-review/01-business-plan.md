# Business plan

## Problem

Volunteer hall managers lose evenings reconciling double bookings by phone (user, D-001).

## Value proposition

For volunteer hall managers, bookings stop colliding, unlike a shared phone diary or Hallkeeper (user, D-001; research, Hallkeeper).

## Payer and user

The hall committee pays. Residents and one manager use it (user, D-002).

## Alternatives

- Community halls often use a shared paper or phone diary instead of software (research, Village Halls Weekly).
- Hallkeeper sells per-venue subscriptions aimed at commercial event spaces (research, Hallkeeper pricing page).

## Market notes

Small venues call the category "venue booking software" (research, Hallkeeper).

## Revenue model

The hall committee pays; residents and one manager use the product (user, D-002). The committee does not charge for the product (assumption, A-001).

## Success metrics

The MVP is request, approve, and cancel for single bookings (user, D-003).

## Assumptions

- A-001: the committee does not charge for the product.
