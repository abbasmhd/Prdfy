# Business plan

## Problem

Volunteer hall managers lose evenings reconciling double bookings by phone (user, D-001).

## Value proposition

For volunteer hall managers, bookings stop colliding, unlike a shared phone diary or Hallkeeper (user, D-001; research, Hallkeeper).

## Payer and user

The hall committee pays. Residents and one manager use it (user, D-002).

## Alternatives

- Shared phone diary: two volunteers can promise the same slot (research, Village Halls Weekly).
- Hallkeeper: priced per venue for commercial event spaces, which is more than a volunteer-run hall needs (research, Hallkeeper pricing page).

## Market notes

Small venues call the category "venue booking software" (research, Hallkeeper).

## Revenue model

Not applicable: the committee does not charge for the product (assumption, A-001).

## Success metrics

No double booking is confirmed during the MVP (user, D-003).

## Assumptions

- A-001: the committee does not charge for the product.
